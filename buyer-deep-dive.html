Here's how it works

A walk-through of every step from search to keys — so you know exactly what to expect when you buy a home on Abode.

1

Start your search

Know that you'll be dealing directly with sellers who have chosen not to pay agency fees. So if you've already signed a realtor's agreement, you will be required to pay their commission yourself.

2

Sign up

Browsing homes does not require an account, saving favorites does. There are no charges until you're ready to make an offer.

3

View properties

Our open house alerts will provide you with a list of all open houses in your chosen area. You can also communicate directly with sellers and set up a viewing time from their calendar of availability. Some sellers will require you to have a pre-approval letter for non-open house showings.

4

Make an offer

Abode's tools make the offer process easy. Submit your offer to purchase complete with terms — including purchase price, closing window, due diligence and earnest money amounts, and other pertinent information.

5

Negotiate

The seller may choose to accept your offer or negotiate further. Our offer portal and messaging tools make communication easy. We'll guide you with tips and advice on best practices along the way.

6

Execute a contract

When terms are agreed, Abode will facilitate the transfer of contract documents for electronic signature.

7

Due diligence & earnest money

Depending on the state and your contract terms, you may need to send checks to the seller and/or closing attorney for earnest money or due diligence amounts. We'll prompt you again on best practices. Most banks and closing attorneys will require you to write a physical check for documentation purposes.

8

Inspections

Coordinate with the seller and your home inspector via the messaging portal. Attend the inspection if you choose, and review the report. If you find repairs are needed, use our tools to issue a repair addendum to the seller.

9

Document management

After entering your lender and closing attorney's contact information, we'll automatically distribute your executed contract to them. Contact both offices to see what further information may be required. We'll prompt you with reminders.

10

Set up closing

Agree on a date and time for closing with the seller and closing attorney. Review the closing roadmap and Abode prompts to make sure all your ducks are in a row for a successful closing.

11

Attend closing

Bring your ID, checkbook or certified check to the attorney's office at the scheduled time, and start signing.

12

Collect your keys

Congratulations on your new home purchase! You took charge of the process — made easier by Abode!
