How does it work?

A walk-through of every step from signing up to closing day — so you know exactly what Abode helps you do.

1

Sign up

Create an account to start your listing. Choose what service tier is right for you. You can upgrade tiers at any time.

2

Build your listing

Set up your listing with Abode's easy-to-use suite of templates to effectively market your home.

3

Post your sign

Savvy Seller tier users can order a custom yard sign complete with web listing QR code linkage.

4

Advertise

Your listing is now live on Abode and affiliate sites. Take advantage of our other marketing offers such as open house fliers, mailers and social media post templates.

5

Manage showings

Manage your showings and open houses with our calendar tool and messaging portal. Only verified buyers can request showings.

6

View offers

View and compare offers and negotiate all through Abode's seller dashboard.

7

Go to contract

Once terms are agreed upon, Abode facilitates the transfer of documents for signature and prompts you on the next steps for a successful closing.

8

Due diligence

Manage and track buyer inspections, surveys and appraisals on Abode's closing roadmap tool.

9

Document management

After entering important contact details for critical roles such as your closing attorney's office, Abode will automatically distribute executed contracts. You'll be prompted with reminders for important milestones and tasks prior to closing.

10

Set up closing

Agree on a closing date and time with your buyer and closing attorney's office. Communicate with the attorney's office to ensure all parties have the necessary documents for a smooth closing.

11

Attend closing

Show up and sign! You've just completed a successful sale and likely saved thousands by putting in the legwork — made easy by Abode!
