Getting started

What is Abode?

Abode is a real estate platform that lets buyers and sellers transact directly — without paying agent commissions on either side. We provide listing tools, contract templates, document management, and a guided closing process so you can complete a home sale or purchase yourself with confidence.

Is Abode a real estate brokerage or agent?

No. Abode is a technology platform, not a brokerage. We don't represent either side of the transaction. We provide the tools, templates, and educational resources you need to complete a transaction on your own — and you keep the right to hire an attorney or agent at any time.

What states does Abode operate in?

Abode is currently available in North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Tennessee, and Virginia. We're rolling out to additional states throughout 2026. Some features — like contract templates — vary by state and locality.

Do I need an account to browse homes?

No. Anyone can browse listings, view photos, see property history, and explore the Education Center without creating an account. You'll only need one once you're ready to schedule a showing, save homes, or submit an offer.

For buyers

How much does it cost to make an offer?

$30 — and that single fee covers up to 3 offers. If none of your offers are accepted within 90 days, the fee is fully refunded automatically.

Do I need to be pre-approved before making an offer?

We strongly recommend it. Most sellers prefer offers backed by a pre-approval letter, and many won't consider offers without one. The Education Center's Mortgages page explains how to get started.

How do I schedule a showing?

On any property page, click "Request a showing" and pick a time from the seller's available windows. The seller confirms it from their dashboard, and you'll both see the booking on the shared calendar in your buyer dashboard.

Can I still hire a real estate attorney?

Absolutely — and we recommend you do. While Abode provides contract templates reviewed by licensed attorneys, every transaction is unique. A local attorney can review the specific terms and answer state-specific questions before you sign.

What happens if my offer is countered?

You'll see the counter-offer in your Negotiation Room. From there you can accept the counter, send a counter of your own, or decline. The full back-and-forth history is preserved for transparency, and the seller will be notified each time you respond.

For sellers

How much will I save selling through Abode?

A typical 6% commission on a $500,000 home is $30,000. Abode's flat listing fees range from $49 to $1,499 depending on the tier you choose. On most sales, that's $25,000 – $29,500 you keep in your pocket. Try the savings calculator on the home page to estimate your own number.

How is my home marketed?

All tiers list on Abode and our affiliated property sites. Savvy Seller ($499) and above also include a custom yard sign and access to optional marketing add-ons like open house flyers, postcards, and 3D Matterport tours. See the Pricing page for tier details.

When do I pay the listing fee?

At closing. You don't pay anything upfront, and if your home doesn't sell, you owe nothing. Every seller tier also includes a 60-day money-back guarantee — even if you decide to switch to a traditional agent partway through.

Can I still work with a real estate agent?

Yes. Some buyers will want to work with an agent — and you can choose to pay that agent's commission as part of the deal, or decline. You're never locked into Abode; the 60-day money-back guarantee covers that scenario.

How long does it take to list my home?

Most sellers complete the listing flow in about 25 – 40 minutes. You can save your progress at any point and come back later. Once you publish, your home appears on Abode immediately and syndicates to partner sites within 24 hours.

Pricing & refunds

What's the difference between the seller tiers?

Basic ($49) covers a standard listing template. Savvy ($499) adds MLS syndication, market analytics, the contract & offer dashboards, and a custom yard sign. Professional ($899) adds step-by-step guidance and physical marketing materials. Investor ($1,499) is built for high-volume sellers with multiple properties. Full side-by-side comparison on the Pricing page.

When is the buyer's $30 fee charged?

When you submit your first offer. The single $30 fee covers up to three submitted offers. If none of them are accepted within 90 days, the fee is refunded to your original payment method automatically.

What's the refund policy?

Sellers get a 60-day money-back guarantee on their listing fee — no questions asked, even if you change your mind and hire an agent.

Buyers get a full refund of the $30 offer fee if no offer is accepted within 90 days.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The seller tier fee or buyer offer fee is the only money Abode collects. Closing costs (title insurance, recording fees, attorney fees, inspections, etc.) are paid directly to the providers of those services — and those costs are the same as in any traditional real estate transaction.

Safety, privacy & legal

Are the contracts on Abode legally binding?

Yes. Once both parties sign, the contract is a binding agreement under the laws of your state. Abode's templates are reviewed by licensed attorneys in each state we operate in. A contract becomes binding only when both parties have signed — and you can always have a local attorney review it before you do.

How does Abode prevent housing discrimination?

Buyer names and photos are hidden from sellers in the offer dashboard. Offers are compared strictly on financial and contractual terms. When two offers are functionally identical, Abode recommends accepting whichever was received first. Read more on the Discrimination in Real Estate page.

Is my personal information protected?

Yes. Buyer identity is not revealed to sellers until you submit an offer. Seller identity is not revealed to the general public — only to buyers who submit an offer. Financial documents (pre-approvals, proof of funds, etc.) are stored encrypted and shared only with parties you explicitly authorize during an active transaction.

Do I need to do anything special at closing?

In most states a closing attorney or title agent handles the final paperwork. Abode provides a closing roadmap that walks you through every step — from clear-to-close through deed recording. The Closing Attorneys & Agents page in the Education Center covers what to expect.
