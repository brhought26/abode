Step 1 · Proof of funds

Upload a pre-approval letter (if you're financing) or a bank statement (if you're paying cash). The seller won't see anything else until you submit your offer.

⚠️
Protect your sensitive information
Before you upload, redact or black out any of the following from your document:
  • Full Social Security numbers (the last 4 digits are usually enough)
  • Full bank account or routing numbers
  • Driver's license or passport numbers
  • Date of birth, if not required for the document
Sellers only need to verify your financing capacity — not your full identity. You can always provide additional details directly to your closing attorney later.
ℹ️
One active offer at a time
While this offer is live, Abode will not let you submit a new offer on another property. You can withdraw an offer to free up the slot, or wait for it to expire or be resolved.
Need to make offers on multiple homes at once? Upgrade to the Investor tier ($150/yr) for unlimited concurrent offers. Requires proof of funds and standard identity verification.
